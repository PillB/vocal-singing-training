### Who we compete with, and why people pick them

- **Free Spanish YouTube is where a Peruvian beginner starts.** One Spanish
  vocal-coach channel, CECI DOVER, has 2.74M subscribers. People pick it
  because it is free, in Spanish and endless. It gives no feedback on the
  learner's voice.
- **Song and karaoke apps** (Yousician, Simply Sing, Smule, StarMaker) are
  picked for real songs, scoring and fun, backed by big brands and 10M–500M
  Android installs. Yousician's singing course and Simply Sing are in English
  only, and Spanish reviewers mostly complain about paywalls.
- **Free pitch monitors** (Nail the Pitch, Vocal Pitch Monitor) are picked
  because they are free and immediate. They show pitch and teach nothing.
- **Spanish-language trainers** (Sing Sharp, VocalGYM, Erol, "Clases Canto",
  Vox Tools, Ella – Canto) are picked for Spanish. Most are small, video-only or
  priced at or above US parity in Peru.
- **On the speaking side**, the coach with by far the most Peru ratings
  teaches English pronunciation (ELSA, 5.2K; BoldVoice is the other accent
  app), and the main Spanish speaking coach is Vocal Image (91 Peru ratings,
  1M+ Android installs). The coaches that measure delivery (Orai, Speeko,
  Poised, Microsoft Speaker Coach, Credible) work in English only. General AI
  (ChatGPT, Gemini, both with free voice modes in Spanish) is the new free
  conversation partner.
- **People** are the real price anchor: a Lima singing teacher averages
  S/ 45–66 an hour, an academy S/ 200–225 a month, and Toastmasters gives a live
  audience. People pick them for a human ear.

Peru facts that shape the comparison:
- Android is **85.64%** of mobile OS share in Peru, iOS 14.36% (Sep 2026,
  [StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/peru)). Most
  price evidence comes from the App Store, which reaches about one phone in seven.
- Google Play in Peru takes cards and carrier billing through **Claro (postpaid
  only) and Entel**
  ([Google help, PE](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2651410?hl=es-419&co=GENIE.CountryCode%3DPE));
  the Peru App Store takes cards and Apple balance
  ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/es-lamr/111741)). None of the apps below
  mentions Yape, which had 16.4M monthly active users in Q1 2026
  ([Infomercado, citing Credicorp](https://infomercado.pe/credicorp-yape-supera-los-mas-de-16-millones-de-usuarios-activos-mensuales/)).
- The minimum wage is **S/ 1,230** a month from 1 Oct 2026
  ([Canal N](https://canaln.pe/actualidad/gobierno-incrementa-remuneracion-minima-vital-1230-soles-n495337)).
- Most singing and speaking apps do **not** localize Peru prices: Sing Sharp,
  VocalGYM, Speeko and Credible charge Peruvians 113–133% of the US price (raw,
  IGV not removed). Yousician (about 30%), Singing Coach AI (58%) and BoldVoice
  (64%) are the exceptions found.

### Gaps we can own

Each gap names its evidence; the rows above carry the links.

1. **Technique explained in Spanish, with feedback on your own voice.**
   Yousician's singing course is English only (its support page); Simply Sing,
   Riyaz, Vocalista and The Vocal Coach are English only in the Peru store. The
   Spanish options are video only (YouTube, "Clases Canto") or small (VocalGYM
   10k+ Android installs, Ella – Canto 18 Peru ratings). A Peru review of
   Vocalista says they would pay if it were in Spanish.
2. **Real practice before paying.** Paywalls are the most repeated complaint in
   Spanish reviews (Simply Sing, Yousician, Riyaz, StarMaker, and an Android
   "Entrenador vocal" that moved to paid). Free alone is not the gap: Nail the
   Pitch, Vocal Pitch Monitor, "Clases Canto" and Ella – Canto are free. Free,
   taught, Spanish and with feedback together is.
3. **Scores people can trust.** After billing, the top complaint is scores that
   are unexplained or wrong: Orai does not explain them, ELSA and BoldVoice
   mark correct words wrong, Vocal Image marks
   words red without guidance, and a Vocaberry user got 0% while on the note.
   Process metrics that show how they are derived (`docs/00-STRATEGY.md` §3.3)
   answer that complaint.
4. **Spanish speaking practice that measures the voice.** Orai, Speeko, Poised,
   Speaker Coach and Credible measure delivery in English only. The
   Spanish-capable options converse (ChatGPT, Gemini, Duolingo, Yoodli roleplay)
   but do not measure pitch or volume. The one exception found, VirtualSpeech,
   measures pace in Spanish roleplays at $45 a month.
5. **Singing and speaking in one place.** No singing app checked has pause,
   pace or diction drills; VocalGYM has no speaking features; Vocal Image is a
   separate speaking subscription.
6. **Voice care in Spanish.** No Spanish consumer app for adult voice care was
   found, and vocal-health guidance shows up on few listings (Sing Sharp, The
   Vocal Coach, the two "Entrenador vocal" apps, Acayo, Vocal Image). We do not
   cover it yet either: the guide has no voice-safety section (red-team audit,
   2026-10-28).
7. **Billing people can trust.** Charged-after-trial or after-cancelling
   reports for Smule, Yousician, Vocal Image, ELSA and BoldVoice; Singing
   Carrots hides its prices until a quiz is done. Peruvian law already requires
   cancelling by the same means as signing up (`docs/35-PRICING.md`).
8. **No install on a cheap Android phone.** Android is 85.64% of Peru; the
   leaders weigh 113–450 MB on iOS (StarMaker 392.7 MB); a Swiftscales buyer
   reports paying again after changing phones. Not unique: Singing Carrots,
   Yoodli, VirtualSpeech, Speaker Coach and Musicca also run in a browser.
9. **Audio kept on the device.** Simply Sing, Smule, StarMaker, Riyaz,
   Vocalista, Yokee, Orai, ELSA and Wellspoken declare audio linked to identity
   in their privacy labels; `privacy.html` says our audio is processed on the
   device. Not unique: Read Along, Credible, Voice Tools, Voice Analyst, Nail
   the Pitch, Erol, Vox Tools and Pitch Witch say the same or declare no data
   collected.

### What we lack

1. **Real songs with scoring.** Yousician (10,000+ songs), Simply Sing, Smule,
   StarMaker and KaraFun score real songs; we use the learner's own lines.
2. **A human.** Video lessons with a face (Sing Sharp, Erol, The Vocal Coach,
   YouTube), feedback on recordings (Singing Carrots' Guided tier; Lima
   teachers at S/ 45–66 an hour) and a live audience (Toastmasters).
3. **High voices.** Our pitch search covers about 65–400 Hz
   (`js/pitch-visualizer.js`); the red-team audit found 420–784 Hz reads an
   octave low and higher notes worse, and every default is male. Simply Sing,
   KaraFun ("Vocal Match") and Moises fit songs to the user's range.
4. **Automatic filler detection, transcripts, words per minute.** Orai, Speeko,
   Poised, Credible and Speaker Coach count fillers on their own; our drill asks
   the learner to tap (`fillerDetect` in `js/practice-profiles.js`). No coach
   found handles Spanish fillers such as "este" or "o sea".
5. **Conversation and roleplay** (Yoodli, VirtualSpeech, Speeko, ELSA, ChatGPT
   voice): interviews, hard conversations, Q&A.
6. **Feedback beyond pitch**: Vocalista's breathiness and resonance notes,
   StarMaker's radar chart, Pitch Witch's formants, Voice Tools' "voice
   gender". Trans voice training (Pitch Witch, Eva, Voice Tools, Christella) is
   an audience we do not serve.
7. **Native-app habits and offline**: push reminders and streak calendars
   (VocalGYM, The Vocal Coach, "Clases Canto"); offline use (KaraFun Premium,
   "Clases Canto", Voice Analyst, Prana Breath, Read Along). The site has no
   service worker, so it needs a connection to load.
8. **Teacher and choir tools**: Singing Carrots for Schools & Choirs, EarMaster
   Cloud, MakeMusic Cloud ($39.99 per teacher a year); Nail the Pitch says voice
   teachers use it.
9. **Store-native payment rails**: carrier billing on Google Play (Claro
   postpaid, Entel). Our rails are in `docs/35-PRICING.md`.
10. **Declared accessibility**: KaraFun declares 7 accessibility features and
    Speeko 6; most leaders declare none. We have ARIA markup (99 `aria-*`
    attributes in `index.html`) but no accessibility audit was found.

### What this suggests for the roadmap (ideas, not commitments)

- Lead with **Spanish technique plus feedback on your own voice plus free
  practice**. "Free", "browser" and "bilingual" are each matched by someone
  above; the combination is not.
- Show **how each score is derived**, in the app and in any listing copy; it is
  the market's top complaint after billing.
- **Fix high voices before reaching for women, children or choirs**: widen the
  pitch search, then consider a voice-type choice.
- A short **voice-safety section** in the guide; vocal-health copy is rare in
  this market and missing from ours.
- **Spanish filler detection** ("este", "eh", "o sea") would be new in the
  market. It needs speech recognition, and any route to it has to fit what
  `README.md` and `privacy.html` already say about audio.
- **Range-fit suggestions** for the learner's own songs (a key shift, as Moises
  and Simply Sing do), without building a song catalogue.
- **Offline loading** for patchy mobile data, and a check of page weight
  against 113–450 MB apps.
- **Teacher sharing**: a teacher assigns an exercise and sees the results, as
  EarMaster and MakeMusic do for schools.
- **Read the Android side first**: Spanish Play reviews are the loudest Peruvian
  voice, and Play hides in-app prices, so App Store comparisons cover only about
  14% of phones.
- **Watch the price points next to ours** (`docs/35-PRICING.md`): ELSA monthly,
  Vocal Image's one-month item, ChatGPT Go and Voice Analyst all sit at S/ 19.90.
- **Avoid "only" claims**: "the only free tool", "the only private one" and "the
  only browser one" each have counter-examples above.

## AI-agent pitfalls avoided
- Too many peer Starts → one **Start practice**
- Same pitch game on all drills → per-exercise modes
- Fake talent scores → process metrics + self-rate
- English-only shell → **ES default + EN toggle**

## Localization (best practices applied)
- Default **es** (LatAm/Perú, clear international Spanish, no slang)
- Toggle shows language **in its own name** (English / Español) — no flags
- `localStorage` persistence; `html[lang]`
- Parity of chrome strings via `data-i18n`

## Hold detector grace
- `HOLD_GRACE_MS` = 900ms in `js/practice-engine.js` (≈ 550ms when this note was first written): brief RMS/pitch dropouts continue the same hold
- `SILENCE_END_MS` = 1100ms (was ≈ 900ms) before logging end
- Both grow with mic sensitivity above 5: +40ms and +50ms per step
- Once holding, energy-only can continue (pitch can flake mid-sustain)
- Hold clock keeps running through grace (retroactive continuity)
